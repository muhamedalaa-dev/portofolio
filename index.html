I'm Mohamed Alaa — a front-end developer based in Cairo, Egypt. Most of my work has been for restaurants, cafés and service businesses around El Shorouk: sites people actually call, book a table on, and order from. No page builders, no bloated frameworks — just hand-written HTML, CSS/Tailwind and JavaScript, shipped fast and built to work in Arabic and English from day one.
